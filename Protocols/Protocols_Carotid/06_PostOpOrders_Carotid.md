First, start by select all outpatient medications in the Active, then "Copy to New Order".
Note: this will restart all **outpatient/home antihypertensive medications**. Only exception to restarting home antihypertensive medications would be patients who have required vasopressors after unclamping to maintain systolic blood pressure >100 mmHg. 

Admit: Admit to 5A

Most orders, including pain medications, are the same as for the [default ("all patient") orders](https://nealbarshes-protocols.github.io/Protocols/Protocols_AllPatients/07_PostOpOrders_AllPatients/)


### Under Surgery Orders --> Vascular Surgery Orders:

FROM LEFT-MOST COLUMN:  
Fall Risk Precautions --> click both to document Precautions and Safety Measures.  
Call physician if: 
  SBP <100mmHg or >140mmHg despite more than two doses of PRN antihypertensives  
  DBP >100mmHg  
  pulse <55 or >120  
  temp >100.4F  
  UOP N/A  
Neuro vitals: Neuro checks q1h for 4 hours, then q4h for 16 hours  
OOB with assistance; modify and add "by 7pm tonight [DAY/DATE]".  
NPO now  

FROM MIDDLE COLUMN:  
Select "Groin Check for Hematoma", then Change to "Check [SIDE] neck and [SIDE] groin (if TCAR) for hematoma every two hours x6 hours. Immediately report any hematoma to staff surgeon or senior trainee."  
Sequential Compression Devices  
Normal Saline: 0.5mL/kg/hour x12 hours  
Other Patient Care Orders --> Text Order Only --> "Inform M.D. if any headache is reported."  

FROM RIGHT COLUMN:  
Aspirin 81mg (Chewable) PO Every Day  
For TCARs: also clopidogrel 75 mg po daily  

In active orders, CEA only: CBC w/ plt, PT/INR & PTT  


### Then Medications, Inpatient:  
labetalol inj, soln; 5mg/1mL INTRAVENOUS (NOT IV piggyback) q1H PRN; Comments: "FIRST LINE for systolic blood pressure >140mmHg"  
metoprolol inj; 5mg/5mL; IV PUSH; q2H PRN; Comments: "SECOND line for systolic blood pressure >140mmHg"  
metoprolol tartrate immediate release tab; 12.5mg ORAL; q3H PRN; Comments: "THIRD line for systolic blood pressure >140mmHg"  
Note: AVOID hydralazine due to potential for cerebral vasodilation; beta-blockers and calcium channel blockers are preferred.  

NPO or clears (Barshes) while in PACU; if neck without swelling, advance to clear liquids or solids (Barshes) when transferred out of PACU. Solid food (regular or diabetic diet) POD #1 at latest unless concerns about nerve injury.
