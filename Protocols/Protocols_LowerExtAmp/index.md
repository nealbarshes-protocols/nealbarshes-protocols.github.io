### Pre-operative preparation:

Read about [pre-operative evaluation for patients undergoing leg amputation](https://nealbarshes.github.io/tutorials/leg-amputation/#pre-operative-planning).

In addition to the typical pre-op workflow, order and review an **x-ray at the planned level of amputation** to ensure there is no orthopedic or other hardware at that level. Higher likelihood of completion if it is either (1) ordered as a portable (bedside) x-ray exam; (2) or ordered to be done in radiology and paired with a text order for a wheelchair escort to get the patient to the radiology department.

The consent form must amputation at the appropriate (planned) level. In addition, please add:
**“Nerve - Implantation of Nerve into Bone or Muscle of Foot”**. This describes the creation of regenerative peripheral nerve interfaces, done to reduce neuroma formation and post-operative phantom limb pains.

### Early post-op
Remove staples and sutures at 6 weeks from the time of surgery. 

### Late follow-up:
Generally done in PM&R Amputee Clinic, which is a walk-in clinic. No scheduled follow-up with us for that ipsilateral limb.
