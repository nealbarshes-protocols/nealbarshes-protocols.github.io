### Roles, duties and expectations of the Night Float Intern: 

Complete post-operative checks (with notes) on all patients who have come out of the operating room after 3pm.

Seeing and evaluating vascular surgery inpatients and existing/active consults with new problems, then directly communicating findings to the on-call senior resident and/or staff surgeon. 

Seeing [intern-level consults](https://nealbarshes-protocols.github.io/Workflows/Consults.html#intern-level-consults), discussing directly with the on-call staff surgeon, and following [all other consult workflow procedures](https://nealbarshes-protocols.github.io/Workflows/Consults.html).

Assisting the on-call senior resident with the evaluation of other consults by gathering data. It is NOT the role of the night intern to act as a scribe for the senior resident. 

Writing OCL History/Physical notes for patients getting admitted to the Vascular Surgery inpatient service.

In the morning, summarizing overnight clinical events to members of the Red and Blue vascular surgery teams.

Please do **NOT**: 
1. Enter lab orders for every inpatient every day. Leave the ordering of labs to the daytime intern.
