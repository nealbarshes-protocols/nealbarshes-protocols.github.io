This describes our team’s process for transitioning clinical responsibilities when fatigue is an issue. 

## When to transition clinical care

Transitioning clinical responsibilities is **mandatory** for surgical trainees when:
1. fatigued
2. additional clinical work would exceed 320 hours during any consecutive four week period

## How to transition clinical care
1. The senior trainee notifies the on-call staff surgeon.
2. Handoff should be given to the on-call staff surgeon and the night float resident using the I-PASS acronym (see below).
3. On-call staff surgeon will inform other staff surgeons.
4. At 6am the following morning, the night float resident should then contact the incoming senior trainee to round on the patients of the senior surgical trainee that stayed late.
5. This senior surgical trainee should come in 10 hours from leaving the hospital (ex. 10am if left at midnight, noon if left at 2am).


The I-PASS acronym for hand-offs of clinical responsibilities:

Letter | Component 
------------ | ------------- 
I= |	Illness severity: stable, "watcher", unstable
P= |	Patient summary
A= |	Action list
S= |	Situational awareness
S= |	Synthesis by receiver
