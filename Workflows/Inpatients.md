## After Rounds (priority order) 

1. Important orders first (consults, imaging, labs, discharges).
2. Communicate with other teams (Teams versus calling).  
3. Wound care not completed on rounds.  
4. Progress notes.
5. Pre-op checklists for pending OR patients.
6. Scrub into OR.
7. Sign out to night intern: 6:00 PM (bring a printed list).

**Core rule**: Never keep critical information to yourself. Call the chief, fellow, or attending early. Honesty and closed-loop communication define a successful rotation.
