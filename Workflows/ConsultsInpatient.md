This workflow applies to patients currently who are admitted to another inpatient service (ex. inpatient/general medicine) and have an inpatient location. This does NOT pertain to E.R. consults not yet admitted or to outpatient consults seen in the hospital building (ex. ECL Wound Care clinic).

### Checklist for the INITIAL consult
1. Ensure the consulting clinician has entered a CPRS consult for our team.   
2. Start a stopwatch to note the time.   
2. Evaluate the patient.   
3. Discuss with the on-call staff surgeon (found on the [schedule](https://dvagov-my.sharepoint.com/:x:/g/personal/neal_barshes_va_gov/ESRAqVRn2DBIjt3UjByYPWoBuwdnmQ7z4zldQHDqAxmKWQ?e=spYbAW))   
4. Add patient information on the "Consults" tab of the [inpatient list](https://dvagov-my.sharepoint.com/:x:/g/personal/neal_barshes_va_gov/ET6QmP7q2EhNgUnzmBfik5kB5QQhS9TTqMkWnsGOlgbreg?e=1E1lkR).   
6. Write a consult note that explains the working diagnosis and management plan.   
   a. Click on patient's location (to the right of name), select "New Encounter", then find and select "HOU VAS INPT ENCS PRV".   
   b. Document important findings   
   c. State and justify the working diagnosis   
   d. Describe the management plan.   
7. Complete the encounter information for the consult note.   
   a. Visit type, Type of Visit = "CONSULTATIONS-INPT". Section name will typically be "Low MDM or 45+ min 99253", "Mod MDM or 60+ min 99254", or "High MDM or 80+ min 99255"   
   b. Make a Service Connected choice: Yes/No. For vascular problems, always "No" for Agent Orange and Military Sexual Trauma.   
   d. Add the on-call staff surgeon to the providers and designate him/her as "Primary".   
   e. Use the most accurate and specific diagnosis that applies, using guidance from the disease-specific webpages here.
